## 📊 **Trust Score Interpretation**

The platform generates a **Trust Score (0-100%)** that combines multiple signals:

### **Score Ranges:**
- **🟢 75-100% (Low Risk):** Document shows strong authenticity indicators
- **🟡 40-74% (Review):** Mixed signals - recommend manual review
- **🔴 0-39% (High Risk):** Strong concerns about authenticity

### **How the Score is Calculated:**

The Trust Score combines three key factors:

1. **Typing Activity (Provenance)**
   - Percentage of content typed by the student vs. pasted
   - Higher typing percentage = higher trust

2. **Authorship Verification (Stylometry)**
   - Compares writing style to student's enrolled baseline samples
   - Verifies consistency with the student's known writing patterns

3. **AI Content Detection**
   - Analyzes text for AI-generated content (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
   - Includes 10% "safe harbor" to account for minor AI assistance

**Formula:** Trust = f(Typing Activity, Authorship Match, AI Detection)
