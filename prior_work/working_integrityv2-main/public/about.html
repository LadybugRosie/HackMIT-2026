About Editorrah

Editorrah is on a mission to make academic writing impossible to cheat. By tracking every keystroke, paste, and pause, we provide universities with tamper-proof proof of authorship—protecting genuine students and exposing misuse of AI or plagiarism tools.

Why we built it

Traditional plagiarism detectors miss up to 50% of AI-generated content. False accusations harm innocent students, while determined cheaters still slip through. We believe provenance—how something was written—matters more than text similarity.
